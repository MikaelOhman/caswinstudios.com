CaspiTalki

Private family chat, calls and calendar, with location sharing for children that's always visible.

CaspiTalki is a private group app for a family, set up by a parent. One phone is the main and owns the group; the others join it by scanning a QR code and checking a safety code on both phones.

What you can do

  • Chat one-to-one and as a group, with photos, replies and reactions
  • Call each other by voice, even to a locked phone
  • Share a family calendar with reminders
  • A parent can see where a child is on a map, only when the child agrees

Private by design

There are no accounts, phone numbers or email addresses. Everything is end-to-end encrypted on the phones before it leaves them; the relay in between only ever sees scrambled data. Chat history stays on the phones, and you can save it to an encrypted backup with a passphrase. An optional app lock uses your phone's own screen lock. No ads, no analytics and no Google services, and CasWin Studios runs no servers for it.

Location sharing is always visible

CaspiTalki includes location monitoring of children by their parent, and it is never hidden:

  • Only members the parent marks as children can be asked; adults' location is never shared
  • Nothing is shared until the child taps Allow on their own phone
  • While sharing, the child's phone shows a permanent notification with a Stop button, and it says when the parent is looking at the map
  • Stopping always tells the parent, and sharing never restarts on its own after a restart
  • Only the latest position is kept, on the parent's phone; there is no location history

If the main phone is lost

The main role can be handed to another phone, and a recovery code (and optional backup phones) lets another phone in the group take over.

CaspiTalki is in Swedish and English and runs on Android.
