BrainUp: Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

BrainUp is made by CasWin Studios, Sweden.

  • BrainUp does not collect, share or sell any personal data.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not ask for internet access and does not send anything off your phone.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Your photos

In Mina nära and the jigsaw puzzle you can choose photos from your phone. You pick each one yourself in the phone's own file picker; the app gets no other access to your photos. A smaller copy of each chosen photo, with the name you type, is kept only in the app's own storage on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Reading aloud

Text is read aloud by your phone's own built-in voice.

Not a medical device

BrainUp is for fun and for keeping your mind active. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease and does not replace care. If you email us, we only use your message and address to read and answer it.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
