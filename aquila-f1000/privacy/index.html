Aquila F1000 2026: Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Aquila F1000 2026 is an Android game made by CasWin Studios, Sweden.

  • Aquila F1000 2026 does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your username, stats, best laps, car setup and settings are stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal information.
  • The game collects no information from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children under 13.

Wi-Fi multiplayer

When you host or join a game on a local network, the game sends your username, chosen driver and car position directly to the other phones in that game. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and nothing is kept after the race. The game uses network permissions only for this.

Tilt steering

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads the phone's motion sensor while you race. This is not stored or sent anywhere.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
