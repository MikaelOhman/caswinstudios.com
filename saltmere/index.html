Saltmere

Fight, loot and build your village on the isle of Caldra.

A fisherman pulls you out of the sea, and you remember nothing. That night goblins come over the fence. Every fight out in the wilds feeds the village: what you loot becomes wood, stone, gear and gold, and the people you rescue come home to live in Saltmere.

A whole story, three endings

Chapter One takes you to the capital. Chapter Two, the Drowned Warden, brings back who you were, and Chapter Three, the Tower in the Snow, ends it. Your choices lead to one of three endings, and the ending changes the island for good. You can keep playing afterwards.

The Lantern Pact

Every people and guild on Caldra has its own questline that ends with them joining the Lantern Pact. How many stand with you at the end changes the final scene.

Your hero, your build

Play a Warrior, a Wizard or a Summoner with a joystick, auto-aim, skill buttons and a dodge roll. Random loot, gear for every class, skill trees and paths let you build your hero your way. Each land has its own levels, up to 30, and Renown beyond. Fights now have proper spell and weapon effects, and fallen foes tumble and go limp, never gory.

Plan a living village

From a bird's-eye view, place homes, fields, fishing huts, workshops, walls, towers and gates. Villagers work on their own, following the priorities you set. The ledger shows what every building makes and who makes it, and the mood window shows how everyone feels. Give them tools and gear, and they work faster and fight back.

The peoples of Caldra

Elves, dwarves, halflings, trolls, fae, spirits and even clockwork folk live on the island. Win their friendship with quests and trade, and some come to live in Saltmere. Each people wants its own kind of home and cares about its neighbours: halflings love a bakery next door, dwarves want the smithy close. Quarters grow naturally from where you build, and the Liking map shows who is happy where. Your scholars can also research 33 studies in food, crafts and defence.

Danger grows

Goblin camps grow until they raid at night, and outlaws hold captives in their camps. Defend your walls, or take a party of villagers and raid them first. Out on the big island wait towns, waystations, more than 20 delves, homesteads with families in trouble, encounters on the road and named beasts to hunt.

  • Cozy, never gory; no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
  • Plays offline; your saves stay on your device, and you can export them yourself
  • Made for phones, and also plays with keyboard and mouse on a TV or monitor
  • An early version that grows with every update
