Driftwood

Build a cozy floating city on a drowned world. Rescue, sail, trade and explore.

The great waves came after the Skyfall, and the world drowned. You wake up on a bare raft with nothing but a hook and a hat. Tap what floats past to hook it in: wood, plastic, scrap and leaves. Rescue the people drifting by.

From raft to city

Your first goal is the Captain's Quarters: gather enough to build it, and there's room for a crew of twelve. Then found your settlement, and the raft becomes the heart of a floating city. Lay decks on the open sea, raise them level by level, and fill them with homes, galleys, workshops, gardens and lanterns. Everything has weight: spread out heavy machines or put them on pontoons, or your city sits low in the water.

Look after your people

Every survivor has a name, a skill and traits. Some are hard-working, some are grumpy, some can't stand the sea. Give them good homes, hot meals and evenings at the tavern, and they'll work happily, fall in love and raise families. The Jobs view shows every workplace, who works there and who's missing, and you set what matters most: which workplaces fill first, which supplies they get, and when to stop making more.

Power, research and the deep

Build wind turbines and research your way up to radio, diving bells and a submarine. Send the landing boat out to dig on islands and watch it row back with its load.

Sail a drowned Earth

Set your course across a map of the old world: mountain-top towns, a mining town on an oil rig, lighthouse keepers who still light the lamps. Barter for what you can't make, help each place with its great need, and earn their trust. Steer clear of islands, sandbars and wrecks, grow crops for every climate, and watch out for storms, doldrums and dust rain.

A story you can find, if you look

Silent islands, a ghost ship turning circles, the wreck of your own ship, and old cables on the seabed that still whisper the world's last messages. None of it interrupts you: it waits out at sea for when you're curious.

  • A relaxed, long city builder: no timers, ads or in-app purchases
  • Three difficulties, from Relaxed (nobody dies) to Hard
  • Tap to build, drag to look around, pinch to zoom
  • Plays offline; your game is saved on your device
  • Made for phones and tablets
