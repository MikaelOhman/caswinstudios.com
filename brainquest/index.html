BrainQuest

Learn by playing, from age 3 to 18. Everything you need for school, and to manage on your own.

BrainQuest is a Swedish learning app that grows with the child, from preschool to upper secondary school. It follows the Swedish curricula (Lpfö 18 and Lgr22), with practice in the same kinds of tasks as the national tests, and it teaches the everyday life skills you need to live on your own.

Many ways to learn

Words and language, sounds and letters, numbers and shapes, nature and technology, society and the world, life and me, English, art, music, food and exercise, and a question to think about every day. Later come crafts and modern languages. There are around 30 kinds of tasks: sort, order, trace and write letters, number lines, clocks, a little shop, board games against an animal, rhythm and clapping, stories and reading aloud.

Paint, play and read

A painting studio with finger paint, stamps and colour mixing, a colouring book, a music studio, and a library of classic books to read or listen to, with the words lit up as they're read.

Made for every child

Simple settings adapt the app: Lugn (calm, for autism and sensitive senses), Fokus (ADHD, with movement breaks), Läsa lätt (dyslexia), Räkna lätt (dyscalculia) and Motorik (motor skills). You can also adjust text size, spacing, contrast, motion, sounds, voice speed and rewards. The app never stores a diagnosis, only the settings you choose.

For parents

A parent area behind a PIN shows each child's progress and screen time next to Folkhälsomyndigheten's guidance, and holds the settings and backups. Every instruction is read aloud, so even children who can't read yet can play.
