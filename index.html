About us

CasWin is two names put together: Caspian and Edwin. We're a dad and his two sons who make things for fun: first music as CasWin Records, now games and apps.

The boys are our Chief Banana Officers. They test everything and come up with ideas for what to make next.

And if a review is just plain grumpy? Then it gets a little poem back, so tomorrow can be a better day:

The sea was rough, the deck was grey,
we're sorry Driftwood spoiled your day.
May tomorrow's tide be kind and slow,
with sun on deck and wind to blow.
