Bacchi Tempel

Grow your village around its tavern, and hold five artifacts at dawn.

Up to eight villages share a fantasy island shaped like the real Lidingö, each growing around its Bacchi Tempel: the tavern where villagers eat, drink and heal. Every night the undead camps send out the dead, and every life they take makes the next horde bigger. Break the camps, carry their artifacts home with your hero, and hold five at dawn to win.

The tavern is the heart

The tavern heals your villagers, shoots at enemies and keeps the artifacts you bring home.

Your hero and party

Choose from six heroes with their own skills, and pick which villagers and soldiers follow you out.

Five races, rival villages

Build up your village with five races, eight resources and twenty kinds of buildings, against rival villages in the fog of war. Artifacts can be carried, stolen and dropped, so nothing is safe until dawn.

The island

The map is shaped from the real landscape of Lidingö, lifted out of the Stockholm archipelago and set alone in open sea, with only the hills, coves and forest left.

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
  • Plays offline against the computer
  • For PC and Android
  • An early version, still growing
