Mistwreck

Build a base from the wreck, lift the fog, and hold out through the nights.

A fog drove your ship onto the rocks of an archipelago of 101 islands. Build a base from the wreck, lift the fog zone by zone, and hold out through nights that never stop coming. The further you push toward the Heart of the Mist, the harder the dark pushes back.

Islands, tides and fog

Low-tide paths link the islands and flood again at high tide. Every island has its own hills, streams, cliffs and beaches. Walking into the fog reveals new land, but every zone you open makes the next night's raid bigger, so you choose how fast to grow.

Your captain

Pick a man or a woman from five peoples (human, halfling, dwarf, wood elf or bog troll) and one of three classes. Your captain levels up and grows along three talent paths.

Towers, traps and a town

Each people has its own blueprints: towers with upgrade paths, traps, walls, homes and workshops. Your citizens work, chat and cheer when a night is held.

Caves, barrows and wrecks

Nine kinds of dungeons, each with its own rooms and a boss. Day and night carry on outside while you're in, so don't stay too long.
