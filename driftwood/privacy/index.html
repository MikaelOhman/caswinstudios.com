Driftwood: Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Driftwood is a game by CasWin Studios.

Driftwood does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not ask for internet access and does not send anything off your device.
  • Your saved games and settings (volume, graphics) are stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal information.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Links in the game

The About page in the game has a link to this website and a Send feedback button. They open your own web browser or email app; if you choose to email us, we only use your message and address to read and answer it.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
