Saltmere

Fight, loot and build your village on the isle of Caldra.

A fisherman pulls you out of the sea, and you remember nothing. That night goblins come over the fence. Every fight out in the wilds feeds the village: what you loot becomes wood, stone, gear and gold, and the people you rescue come home to live in Saltmere.

Your hero, your build

Play a Warrior, a Wizard or a Summoner with a joystick, auto-aim, skill buttons and a dodge roll. Random loot, gear for every class, skill trees and paths let you build your hero your way. Each land has its own levels, up to 30, and Renown beyond.

Plan a living village

From a bird's-eye view, place homes, fields, fishing huts, workshops, walls, towers and gates. Villagers work on their own, following the priorities you set. The ledger shows what every building makes and who makes it, and the mood window shows how everyone feels. Give them tools and gear, and they work faster and fight back.

The peoples of Caldra

Elves, dwarves, halflings, trolls, fae, spirits and even clockwork folk live on the island. Win their friendship with quests and trade, and some come to live in Saltmere, each with their own homes and skills. Grow the village into districts: the harbour, the fields, the greenwood and the stone deeps.

Danger grows

Goblin camps grow until they raid at night, and outlaws hold captives in their camps. Defend your walls, or take a party of villagers and raid them first. Out on the big island wait towns, waystations, more than 20 delves and the long road to the capital.
