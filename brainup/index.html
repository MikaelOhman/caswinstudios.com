BrainUp

Fun little games and everyday challenges that can get your brain going in nice ways.

BrainUp is a Swedish app for older people. It's calm, clear and easy to use: big text you choose yourself, light or dark colours, and everything can be read aloud. There are no timers and nothing you can fail.

Every day

The home screen greets you with the day and date, read aloud, under a small painted landscape that follows the time of day and the season. Each day suggests three small things to do, if you feel like it.

Games

Patience, sudoku in three sizes, word search, jigsaw puzzles (also of your own photos), a quiz, picture pairs, old Swedish sayings and a little everyday challenge. In Mina nära you add photos of the people close to you and practise their faces and names.

Move and train

Pick what you'd like to work on and get small exercises to do away from the screen, such as throwing and catching or balance by the bench. Say whether each one felt easy, just right or hard, and the next one follows.

  • No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no tracking
  • Works fully offline; photos and progress stay on the phone
  • Big text, read aloud, slow or normal speech

BrainUp is for fun and for keeping your mind active. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, and it does not replace care. If you're worried about your memory, talk to your health centre (vårdcentral).
